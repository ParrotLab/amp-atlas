## The problem

> _What's not working today? Who feels the pain, and what does it cost us (time, money, quality, morale)? A concrete example beats an abstract description._

## The proposed approach

> _What are you proposing to do about it? Describe the shape of the solution at a high level — the idea, not every detail._

## Why now

> _Why is this worth doing now rather than later? What changed, or what's the cost of waiting?_

## What success looks like

> _How will you know this worked? Describe the outcome you expect — ideally something you could point to or measure._

> _What's included — and, just as importantly, what's not. Naming what you're not doing keeps the project focused._
