Q2 Content Strategy
Created by Rose · May 3, 2026 Last edited 5 min ago
Problem

Our current content pipeline relies on manual processes that don't scale. Each team member creates documentation in isolation — some in Google Docs, some locally, some in Notion — and there's no single place where the team's knowledge lives and evolves together.

The goal is to centralize our team's content creation into a shared, versioned system where everyone can contribute, review each other's work, and build on what's already been documented.

Success Criteria
  • All new playbooks are created and maintained in the Learning System
  • Every team member can edit and submit content for review without technical help
  • Review turnaround under 24 hours for submitted content
  • Weekly publishing cadence — at least 2 new or updated docs per week across the team
Scope
In Scope
  • Onboarding documentation refresh
  • New sales enablement playbook
  • Process documentation for Q2 initiatives
  • Reference template updates
Out of Scope
  • External-facing content (marketing site, blog)
  • Technical documentation (API docs, architecture)
Team & Roles
  • Rose — Onboarding playbook, process docs Lead
  • Hannah — Sales enablement playbook Lead
  • Rachel — Strategy review, final approval Reviewer
  • Kristi — System setup, template creation Support
