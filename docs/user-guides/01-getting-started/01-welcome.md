# Welcome to AMP Atlas

AMP Atlas is the calm, friendly home for your team's knowledge.

Think of everything your team knows — your processes, your reference material, your
project work, the playbooks you run every week — living in one organized, trustworthy
place that both **you and Claude** can read, edit, and improve together. That's a
**System**. AMP Atlas is where you work inside your Systems.

If your team has ever lost good work in a sea of Google Docs, Slack threads, and
half-remembered Notion pages, AMP Atlas is the answer to that. One organized source of
truth per part of your business — kept current, kept trustworthy, and always reviewed
before it becomes official.

---

## The one big idea: Draft → Review → Publish

Everything in AMP Atlas is safe by default, because of one simple cycle.

- **The Live Version** is the official, published, agreed-upon version of a System.
  It's what everyone on your team sees and works from. It's always trustworthy, because
  nothing lands here without a review.
- When you want to make a change, you make it in a **Draft** — your own private working
  copy. You can edit freely, save as you go, and try things out. The Live Version is
  never touched while you work.
- When your Draft is ready, you **submit it for review**. A teammate reads your changes
  and either approves them or asks for tweaks.
- Once approved, you **Publish** — and your Draft becomes the new Live Version for
  everyone.

```
  Live Version  ──▶  make a Draft  ──▶  edit freely  ──▶  submit for review  ──▶  Publish
   (official)          (yours)          (safe)            (a teammate checks)     (now official)
```

That's the whole rhythm. **Draft → Review → Publish.** Every guide in this handbook is
really just a variation on this loop.

---

## Why it works this way

Two things make AMP Atlas different from a shared folder of documents:

1. **Every change is tracked and reviewed.** Nothing becomes official until a human has
   looked at it. That's what keeps your team's knowledge trustworthy instead of slowly
   drifting out of date.
2. **You and Claude read the same source.** When Claude helps you on a project, it's
   working from the exact same documents you are — not a separate, invisible copy. So
   you can always see what it worked from and check its work.

You get the power of serious version control and team review — the stuff that keeps big,
important knowledge bases reliable — without ever touching a single piece of technical
machinery. AMP Atlas handles all of that quietly underneath.

> **You'll never need to be technical to use AMP Atlas.** There's no code, no commands,
> and no jargon to learn. If you can write a document, you can use AMP Atlas.

---

## What you can do here

- **Organize your team's knowledge** into Systems — one per part of your business
  (Learning, Delivery, Marketing, and so on).
- **Write and edit documents** in a clean, friendly editor.
- **Cowork with Claude** on new projects, then review and refine the results.
- **Submit your work for review** and **review your teammates' work** — the way your
  team keeps quality high.
- **Run several pieces of work at once** without them colliding.

## A quick map of the words

You'll see a handful of AMP Atlas words throughout these guides. Here are the ones worth
knowing on day one:

| Word | What it means |
|------|----------------|
| **System** | A home for one part of your team's knowledge (like a Learning System or Delivery System). |
| **Sub-system** | A smaller area inside a System — a way to divide a big System into its parts. |
| **Live Version** | The official, published version of a System that everyone shares. |
| **Draft** | Your private working copy where you make changes safely. |
| **Publish** | Sending an approved Draft to become the new Live Version. |
| **Review** | A teammate checking your Draft before it's published. |

There's a fuller [glossary](../02-core-concepts/03-glossary.md) when you want it — but these
will carry you a long way.
