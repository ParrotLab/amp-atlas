# A tour of the app

Let's get you oriented. AMP Atlas has just a few main areas, and once you know them,
you'll always know where you are.

---

## The dashboard — your home base

When you open AMP Atlas, you land on the **dashboard**. This is your home screen, and it
shows:

- **Your Systems** — one card each, in the color and icon you chose. Click a card to open
  that System.
- **Jump back in** — a quick way to return to what you were working on most recently, so
  you don't have to go hunting.
- **Recently edited** — the documents you've touched lately, across your Systems.

Whenever you want to get back to neutral ground, the dashboard is home.

---

## The sidebar — moving around

The **sidebar** runs down the side of the app and is always there. It's how you move
between the big areas:

- **Your Systems** — switch from one System to another.
- **Inbox** — reviews waiting on you (more below).
- **Add System** — bring in a new System.
- **Settings** — manage your Systems and your account.

---

## Inside a System — the file tree and editor

When you open a System, you'll see its contents laid out as a **file tree** — the folders
and documents that make up that System, organized in the [standard shape](../02-core-concepts/folder-structure.md).

- Click any document to open it in the **editor**, where you read and write.
- Right-click in the tree to create new documents and folders, or start from a template.

The editor is a clean, friendly writing space — no code, no clutter. There's more on it
in [Editing basics](../03-everyday-workflows/editing-basics.md).

---

## The Inbox — what needs your attention

The **Inbox** is where reviews land when a teammate asks *you* to look at their work. If
someone submits a Draft and picks you as a reviewer, it shows up here. Think of it as your
"needs my eyes" list. Reviewing is covered in
[Reviewing someone else's work](../03-everyday-workflows/review-someones-work.md).

---

## The status bar — where you are right now

Along the bottom (or edge) of the app, a small **status bar** keeps you oriented while
you work. It shows things like:

- Which **Draft** you're currently in (or whether you're viewing the **Live Version**).
- Whether you have unsaved changes.
- Whether **updates are available** from the Live Version.

It's a quick glance to answer "where am I, and is everything current?" — no need to go
digging.

> **A note on the Live Version:** when you're viewing a System's Live Version, the editor
> is **read-only** — you can look but not change it. That's by design: the Live Version is
> official, so edits always happen in a Draft. To make changes, you create or switch to a
> Draft (see [Editing basics](../03-everyday-workflows/editing-basics.md)).

---

**You should now recognize** the dashboard, the sidebar, a System's file tree and editor,
the Inbox, and the status bar — enough to find your way around without getting lost.
