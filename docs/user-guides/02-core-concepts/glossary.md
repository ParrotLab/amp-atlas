# Key words, translated

A quick reference for the words you'll see throughout AMP Atlas. These aren't technical
terms to memorize — they're just the shared vocabulary your team uses to talk about the
work. Skim it now, come back whenever a word isn't clear.

---

## The building blocks

### System
A home for one part of your team's knowledge — like a **Learning System**, a **Delivery
System**, or a **Marketing System**. Each System owns exactly one area of your business
and is the single source of truth for it. When you open AMP Atlas, your Systems are what
you see. _(Think of it as the equivalent of a "vault" or "workspace" if you've used tools
like Obsidian.)_

### Sub-system
A smaller area **inside** a System — a way to divide a big System into its parts. For
example, a Delivery System might have sub-systems for onboarding, support, and grading.
Sub-systems keep a large System organized so it doesn't become one giant pile of
documents.

### Project
A specific, scoped piece of work inside a System — something with a beginning and an end,
like "redesign the onboarding email sequence." Projects hold the messy in-progress work:
the early thinking, the plan, the drafts, and the finished result.

### Playbook
A repeatable process, written down so it can be run the same way every time — by you or
by Claude. A playbook captures "here's exactly how we do this" for recurring work, like
grading a session or drafting a support answer.

## The Draft → Review → Publish cycle

### Live Version
The **official, published, agreed-upon** version of a System. It's what everyone on your
team sees and works from, and it's always trustworthy — because nothing becomes the Live
Version without a review. Nobody edits the Live Version directly.

### Draft
Your **private working copy** where you make changes safely. You can edit, save, and
experiment freely in a Draft without affecting the Live Version or anyone else. When a
Draft is ready, you submit it for review. You can have several Drafts going at once.

### Save
Keeping your progress inside a Draft as you work. Saving does **not** make anything
official — it just records where you are so nothing is lost. (This is different from
Publishing.)

### Publish
Sending an **approved** Draft out to become the new Live Version. Once you Publish, your
changes are official and everyone sees them. Publishing only happens after a review.

### Review
The step where a teammate reads your Draft before it's published and either **approves**
it or **requests changes**. Reviews are how your team keeps quality high and catches
things before they become official. See
[Submitting your work for review](../03-everyday-workflows/submit-for-review.md) and
[Reviewing someone else's work](../03-everyday-workflows/review-someones-work.md).

## How a System is organized

### Reference
The **canonical, approved** material in a System — the stuff that's trusted and used to
do the work. Rubrics, guides, templates, finished frameworks. If something is in
Reference, it's treated as true. Think of it as "the official shelf."

### Work
The **in-progress** material — active projects, drafts, and things still being shaped.
Keeping Work separate from Reference is what stops half-finished material from being
mistaken for the real thing. Think of it as "the workbench."

> **The most important distinction in any System:** _Reference_ is trusted and done;
> _Work_ is in-flight and still being figured out. When in doubt, ask: "Is this finished
> and official, or still being worked on?"

## A few more you'll bump into

| Word | What it means |
|------|----------------|
| **Dashboard** | Your home screen in AMP Atlas — shows all your Systems and where you left off. |
| **Inbox** | Where reviews waiting on *you* show up. |
| **Properties** | The small labeled details attached to a document (like its status or owner). |
| **Status** | A label on a document showing how finished it is — e.g. _draft_, _needs review_, or _approved_. |
| **Updates available** | A signal that the Live Version has changed since you started your Draft, so you may want to pull those updates in. See [When the Live Version changes](../03-everyday-workflows/updates-and-conflicts.md). |
