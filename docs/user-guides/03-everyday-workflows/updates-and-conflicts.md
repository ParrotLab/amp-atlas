# When the Live Version changes

While you're working away in your Draft, the **Live Version** might change underneath you —
because someone else finished their work and published it. This is completely normal on an
active team, and AMP Atlas is built to handle it gently. This guide explains what you'll see
and what to do.

**The short version: nothing you're working on is ever lost.** Read on for the details.

---

## Why the Live Version moves

Remember that the Live Version is the shared, official version everyone works from. When a
teammate (or Claude) gets a Draft approved and **publishes** it, the Live Version updates for
everyone — including you, mid-Draft.

That's a good thing: it means the team's shared knowledge is staying current. It just means
your Draft, which started from an *earlier* Live Version, is now a little behind.

---

## "Updates available"

When this happens, AMP Atlas shows you a gentle **"updates available"** signal (you'll spot
it in the status bar). It's not an error and nothing is wrong — it's just letting you know:
*the Live Version has moved forward since you started, and you can pull those updates into
your Draft when you're ready.*

To catch your Draft up, choose to **update your Draft**. AMP Atlas brings the latest Live
Version changes into your Draft, so you're working from current material again.

> **When should you update?** Any time is fine, but it's a good idea to update before you
> submit for review, so your reviewer sees your work against the current Live Version. If you
> know a big change just published, updating sooner rather than later keeps things simple.

---

## When your Draft and the new updates disagree

Most of the time, updating is instant and invisible — your changes and the new updates are in
different places, so they simply combine. Done.

Occasionally, though, your Draft changed *the same spot* that the new update also changed. AMP
Atlas can't guess which one you want, so it asks you. This is the one moment where you make a
quick decision — and AMP Atlas **guides you through it** rather than leaving you stuck.

For each spot where there's a disagreement, you'll see the two versions side by side:

- **Your version** — what you wrote in your Draft.
- **The updated version** — what came in from the new Live Version.

You just pick which one to keep (or combine them), spot by spot. Once you've made your
choices, your Draft is caught up and you're back to work.

> This sounds more dramatic than it is. It usually happens on just a line or two, it's a
> simple "keep this one" choice, and AMP Atlas walks you through each spot. Take it one at a
> time.

---

## Nothing is ever lost

The most important reassurance: **your work is safe through all of this.**

- Your Draft is yours; updating it doesn't throw away what you wrote.
- If a disagreement comes up, *you* decide what to keep — AMP Atlas never silently
  overwrites your work.
- Even after you resolve things, your Draft's history is intact.

So when you see "updates available," there's no need to worry. It's just AMP Atlas keeping you
in sync with the team.

---

available" means and how to update your Draft, how the guided step works when there's a
disagreement, and — most importantly — that your work is never lost.
