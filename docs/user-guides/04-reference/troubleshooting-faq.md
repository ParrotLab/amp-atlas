# Troubleshooting & FAQ

Quick answers to the things that most often trip people up. If your question isn't here, ask
your team lead or in your team's support channel.

---

## Editing & saving

**"The editor won't let me type — is it broken?"**
Almost always, this means you're looking at the **Live Version**, which is read-only on
purpose. Make your changes in a **Draft** instead: start or switch to a Draft, and you'll be
able to edit. The status bar tells you which one you're in.

**"I made changes — why doesn't the rest of my team see them?"**
Because they're still in your **Draft**, which is private to you. Your team only sees changes
once the Draft has been reviewed and **published** to the Live Version. This is by design —
it's what keeps the Live Version trustworthy. See
[Submitting your work for review](../03-everyday-workflows/submit-for-review.md).

**"What's the difference between Save and Publish again?"**
**Save** keeps your progress inside your Draft (private, not official). **Publish** takes an
approved Draft and makes it the new Live Version (official, seen by everyone). You Save
constantly; you Publish only after a review.

## Drafts & versions

**"Where did my Draft go after I published it?"**
That's expected. Once a Draft is published, its changes *become* the Live Version, and the
Draft has done its job, so it's cleared away. Your work isn't lost — it's now part of the
official Live Version that everyone sees. If you need to make more changes, start a fresh
Draft.

**"What does 'updates available' mean?"**
It means the **Live Version changed** since you started your Draft (someone else published
something). It's not an error. You can pull those changes into your Draft by choosing to
**update your Draft**. Full details in
[When the Live Version changes](../03-everyday-workflows/updates-and-conflicts.md).

**"I have a bunch of Drafts — how do I tell them apart?"**
By their names, and by the **status bar**, which always shows which Draft you're currently
in. Naming Drafts clearly when you create them ("Onboarding refresh," "Rubric fix") makes
this easy. See
[Working on several projects at once](../03-everyday-workflows/working-on-multiple-drafts.md).

## Reviews

**"I'm reviewing a teammate's work and I can't edit it. Why?"**
That's intentional — **you can't edit someone else's Draft.** The Draft belongs to its
author, so your feedback goes through **comments**, and *they* make the changes. Leave
clearly-labeled comments (blocking / nitpick / question / praise) and choose **Approve** or
**Request Changes**. See
[Reviewing someone else's work](../03-everyday-workflows/review-someones-work.md).

**"Someone requested changes on my Draft — do I start over?"**
No. Just keep working in the **same Draft**: make the fixes, save, and the review updates
automatically so your reviewer can take another look. Nothing is lost and nothing restarts.

**"How do I know when something needs my review?"**
It shows up in your **Inbox**. If a teammate picks you as a reviewer, their Draft lands
there.

## Account & connection

**"AMP Atlas is asking me to reconnect to GitHub."**
Your connection dropped (this happens occasionally — a token expiring, for example). Your
local work is fine. Just click to **reconnect** and approve in the browser, the same quick
step as the first time. See
[Set up your account & first System](../01-getting-started/account-and-first-system.md).

**"I approved in the browser but AMP Atlas still says I'm not connected."**
Switch back to the AMP Atlas window and give it a moment. If it still doesn't update, click
**Connect to GitHub** again for a fresh code.

**"Do I need to know how to use GitHub?"**
No. AMP Atlas uses GitHub quietly underneath for version history and reviews, but you never
touch it directly. Connecting once is the only time GitHub comes up.

## Working with Claude

**"Claude changed things — is that safe? Did it touch the Live Version?"**
Safe. Claude works in a **Draft**, exactly like you do, and its work goes through the same
**review** before anything becomes official. Nothing Claude produces reaches the Live Version
without a person approving it. See
[Coworking with Claude](../03-everyday-workflows/coworking-with-claude.md).
