# Video 1 — Setting up your account & systems

**Format:** Loom screen-share of AMP Atlas
**Length:** ~4–5 min
**Goal:** Viewer can open AMP Atlas, connect their account, and add their first System.

---

## Before you record

- 💡 Start **signed out** (or on a fresh machine/profile) so you can show the **Connect** screen.
  If you can't easily sign out, you can start on the dashboard and describe the connect step.
- 💡 Have a **demo System folder** already on your computer and connected to GitHub, ready to add.
- 💡 Know which folder you'll pick, so you're not fishing through Finder on camera.
- 💡 Have your browser ready — the connect step pops it open.

---

## The script

### 0. Cold open ⏱️ ~15s
**🖱️ On screen:** AMP Atlas open on the Connect screen (or the dashboard).
**🎙️ Say:** "Hey! In this quick video I'll show you how to get into AMP Atlas — connecting
your account, and adding your first System, which is the home for one area of your team's
knowledge. It takes about five minutes, and there's nothing technical about it."

### 1. What a System is (set expectations) ⏱️ ~20s
**🖱️ On screen:** Stay on the current screen; gesture as you talk.
**🎙️ Say:** "One thing to know up front: your team's Systems already exist as folders on your
computer, set up ahead of time. So 'adding a System' just means pointing AMP Atlas at one of
those folders — you're connecting to it, not building it. If you've used Obsidian, a System is
basically the same idea as a vault."

### 2. Connect to GitHub ⏱️ ~60s
**🖱️ On screen:**
- Point at the **Connect to GitHub** button.
- Click it. Show the short code that appears.
- Switch to your browser as it opens; enter/confirm the code; approve the connection.
- Switch back to AMP Atlas showing the connected state.

**🎙️ Say:** "First, we connect. AMP Atlas keeps everything safe and reviewable using GitHub
behind the scenes — but you'll never actually *use* GitHub yourself. I'll click **Connect to
GitHub**… it gives me this short code… and opens my browser. I pop the code in, approve it…
and back in AMP Atlas, we're connected. That's a one-time thing on this computer."

**💡 Tip:** Make sure no private repos or account details are visible in the browser when you
switch to it.

### 3. Quick look at the dashboard ⏱️ ~20s
**🖱️ On screen:** The dashboard. Sweep across the "Your systems" area and the "Jump back in" rail.
**🎙️ Say:** "This is the dashboard — your home base. Your Systems show up here as cards, and
over on the right, 'Jump back in' brings you straight back to whatever you were working on.
Right now it's empty, so let's add a System."

### 4. Add your first System ⏱️ ~75s
**🖱️ On screen:**
- Click the **Add system** tile (mention Settings works too).
- In the modal, type a **name**.
- Pick a **color** and an **icon**.
- Click to choose the **folder**, and select your demo System folder.
- Confirm/save.

**🎙️ Say:** "I'll click **Add system** — you can also do this from Settings. I give it a
name… pick a color and an icon so it's easy to spot… and then I point it at the folder on my
computer where this System already lives. I'll choose that folder… and done. Quick note: that
folder needs to be one your team already set up and connected to GitHub — if it isn't, AMP
Atlas will just let you know instead of adding it."

### 5. Adding more Systems ("new system") ⏱️ ~20s
**🖱️ On screen:** Point back at the **Add system** tile.
**🎙️ Say:** "Got more than one System? You add each the exact same way — same **Add system**
button, point it at that System's folder. So whether it's your first System or your fifth,
it's this same quick step."

### 6. Open it up ⏱️ ~25s
**🖱️ On screen:** Click the new System card. Show it opening into the file tree + editor.
**🎙️ Say:** "Now my System's on the dashboard, showing its status and how many playbooks it
has. I'll click in… and there it is — all its documents, ready to work with."

### 7. Wrap ⏱️ ~15s
**🖱️ On screen:** Back on the dashboard, or resting in the System.
**🎙️ Say:** "That's it — you're set up. Next, check out the video on the folder structure to
get your bearings inside a System, and the one on coworking with Claude when you're ready to
start creating. Thanks for watching!"

---

## If something goes wrong while recording

- **The connect code expires** before you approve it — just click **Connect to GitHub** again
  for a fresh one, and pick the recording back up.
- **The browser doesn't switch cleanly** — pause, get to the approved state, and resume on
  "back in AMP Atlas, we're connected."
- **Your folder won't add** — that means it isn't a connected System; switch to a folder you
  know is set up before recording this beat.
