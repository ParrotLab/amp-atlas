# Video 3 — Submitting your work for review

**Format:** Loom screen-share of AMP Atlas
**Length:** ~4 min
**Goal:** Viewer can save a Draft, submit it for review with a reviewer, and knows what happens
after — including that they publish it themselves once it's approved.

---

## Before you record

- 💡 Be in a **Draft** with a few real-looking changes already made and saved.
- 💡 Have at least one **teammate/collaborator** available in the reviewer picker (a demo
  account is fine).
- 💡 If you want to show the "Ready to publish" end state, have an already-approved Draft
  waiting in your Inbox to cut to.

---

## The script

### 0. Cold open ⏱️ ~20s
**🖱️ On screen:** In a Draft with visible edits.
**🎙️ Say:** "So you've done some work in a Draft — now let's get it reviewed and made
official. In this video I'll show you how to submit your work for review, and what happens
after. The quick version is: submit, a teammate approves, and then you publish."

### 1. Save first ⏱️ ~20s
**🖱️ On screen:** Point at the status bar; press **⌘S** / click Save. Show "all changes saved."
**🎙️ Say:** "First I'll make sure everything's saved — I'll hit save, and you can see it
switch to 'all changes saved.' Saving just keeps my progress in the Draft; it doesn't send it
anywhere yet."

### 2. Open Submit for review ⏱️ ~30s
**🖱️ On screen:** Open the Save split-button; choose **Submit for review**. The submit window opens.
**🎙️ Say:** "When I'm ready, I'll open this menu on the Save button and choose **Submit for
review**. That opens this window, which is where I hand it off."

### 3. Walk the submit window ⏱️ ~50s
**🖱️ On screen:**
- Point at the change summary (saves / files changed / recent notes).
- Add a **title**.
- Add a short **description**.
- Open the **reviewer** picker and select a teammate.

**🎙️ Say:** "Up top it shows a quick summary of what I'm publishing — how much changed and my
recent notes, so my reviewer sees the shape of it. I'll give it a clear title… a short
description of what changed and why… and then, importantly, I pick who should review it.
I'll choose my teammate here."

### 4. Ask for the right feedback ⏱️ ~30s
**🖱️ On screen:** In the description field, type a one-line note about what you want checked.
**🎙️ Say:** "One habit that makes reviews way better: tell your reviewer what kind of feedback
you actually want. Is the *structure* still up for grabs, or is it basically done and you just
want the *details* checked? A quick line like 'structure's set, please check the facts' saves
everyone time. That's just something you type — there's no special setting for it."

### 5. Submit ⏱️ ~20s
**🖱️ On screen:** Click **Submit for review**. Show the confirmation / return to the Draft.
**🎙️ Say:** "And I'll submit. That sends it to my teammate — it shows up in *their* Inbox
under 'Needs your review.' My work is still just a Draft for now; nothing's official until it's
approved and published."

### 6. What comes back ⏱️ ~40s
**🖱️ On screen:** Open your **Inbox**; point at the **Your drafts** and **Ready to publish** tabs.
**🎙️ Say:** "Two things can come back. If they **request changes**, I'll get a note about what
to fix — I just make the changes in this same Draft and submit again, no starting over. If they
**approve** it, my Draft shows up in my Inbox under **Ready to publish**."

### 7. Publish it ⏱️ ~30s
**🖱️ On screen:** On the **Ready to publish** tab, click **Publish** (use a pre-approved demo Draft).
**🎙️ Say:** "This last step is mine to take. Approving is my teammate's sign-off — it clears
the way, but *I* publish. I'll click **Publish**… and now my Draft is the new Live Version.
Everyone on the team sees it. Done."

### 8. Wrap ⏱️ ~15s
**🖱️ On screen:** Rest on the dashboard or the System's Live Version.
**🎙️ Say:** "That's the whole path: save, submit for review, get approved, publish. Next up, if
you want to see the other side, check out 'how to review someone else's work.' Thanks!"

---

## If something goes wrong while recording

- **No reviewers appear** in the picker — that means collaborators aren't loading; sort that
  before recording so the pick is smooth.
- **You don't have an approved Draft** to show Publish — narrate step 7 over the Inbox and note
  "once it's approved, the Publish button lives right here," rather than faking it.
- **You realize the Live Version moved** while submitting — mention AMP Atlas catches your Draft
  up automatically at publish, and point viewers to the "when the Live Version changes" guide.
