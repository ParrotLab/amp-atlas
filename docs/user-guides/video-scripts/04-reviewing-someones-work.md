# Video 4 — Reviewing someone else's work

**Format:** Loom screen-share of AMP Atlas
**Length:** ~4–5 min
**Goal:** Viewer can open a review, read the changes, leave feedback as a single note, and
choose Approve or Request changes — and understands they don't edit the author's Draft.

---

## Before you record

- 💡 Have a **review waiting in your Inbox** under "Needs your review" (have a teammate/demo
  account submit a Draft with a couple of changed files).
- 💡 Pick a review with a small, clear change so the diff reads well on camera.
- 💡 Decide whether you'll demo **Approve** or **Request changes** (you can do both on two
  different reviews if you want to show each).

---

## The script

### 0. Cold open ⏱️ ~20s
**🖱️ On screen:** AMP Atlas open, about to go to the Inbox.
**🎙️ Say:** "When a teammate wants your eyes on their work, it comes to you as a review. In
this video I'll show you how to review someone else's Draft — how to read what changed, leave
your feedback, and either approve it or ask for changes. Reviewing is honestly one of the most
valuable things you do here — it's how we keep everything trustworthy."

### 1. Find it in your Inbox ⏱️ ~25s
**🖱️ On screen:** Open **Inbox** → **Needs your review** tab. Click the review to open it.
**🎙️ Say:** "Reviews waiting on me live in the Inbox, under **Needs your review**. I'll open
this one up."

### 2. Read the changes ⏱️ ~60s
**🖱️ On screen:**
- Expand a changed file.
- Toggle between **Updated version** and **What changed**.
- Do it on a second file if helpful.

**🎙️ Say:** "Here are the changed files. For each one I can look two ways: **Updated version**
shows the file as it'll read if we publish it — the finished result — and **What changed**
highlights just the differences, in red and green. I'll flip between them to really understand
what's going on. I take my time here; a good review means actually reading it, not just
skimming."

### 3. Mark each file reviewed ⏱️ ~25s
**🖱️ On screen:** Click **Mark reviewed** on each file; show it become **✓ Reviewed**.
**🎙️ Say:** "As I finish each file, I'll mark it **Reviewed**. AMP Atlas actually makes me
check off *every* file before I can approve — that's on purpose, so nothing sneaks through
unlooked-at."

### 4. The big rule: don't edit their Draft ⏱️ ~35s
**🖱️ On screen:** Gesture at the read-only review view; don't try to type into their content.
**🎙️ Say:** "Now, the most important habit in reviewing: I do *not* go in and edit my
teammate's Draft. It's their work — if reviewers started changing each other's Drafts, we'd
lose track of who decided what. So my job is to give feedback and let *them* make the changes.
I'm a reviewer here, not an editor."

### 5. Leave your feedback (one note on the whole review) ⏱️ ~55s
**🖱️ On screen:**
- Point at the note field in the action bar.
- Type a short, structured note.

**🎙️ Say:** "My feedback goes in one note, right here — think of it as a comment on the whole
review. There aren't line-by-line comments in the app; it's this single note plus my decision.
So I'll keep it clear and organized. A trick that helps: label each point so the author knows
what's essential. I'll write something like — 'Structure looks great. Blocking: the score in
step three is wrong. Nitpick: the intro could be tighter. Praise: love the examples.' Those
labels are just how I word the note — it tells them exactly what must change versus what's
optional."

**💡 Tip:** Keep your example note visible long enough for viewers to read it.

### 6. Make your decision ⏱️ ~40s
**🖱️ On screen:** Point at **Approve** and **Request changes**. Click the one you're demoing.
**🎙️ Say:** "Then I decide. **Approve** means it's good to become official — a note's optional
there. **Request changes** means something needs fixing first — and there a note's required, so
they know what. Heads up: approving is a *sign-off*, not publishing — it clears the way, and
then the author publishes it themselves. I'll [approve this / request changes here]… and that
sends it right back to them."

### 7. Wrap ⏱️ ~15s
**🖱️ On screen:** Back to the Inbox.
**🎙️ Say:** "That's a review: read it carefully, give one clear note, and approve or ask for
changes — without editing their Draft. That back-and-forth is what keeps our work sharp.
Thanks for watching!"

---

## If something goes wrong while recording

- **Approve is greyed out** — you haven't marked every file reviewed yet; that's actually a
  nice thing to point out on camera.
- **You want to show both outcomes** — record Approve on one review and Request changes on
  another, then use whichever fits (or keep both as a longer video).
- **You're tempted to fix a typo yourself** — don't; that's the exact anti-pattern this video
  teaches. Put it in the note instead.
