# Video 5 — Working on several projects at once

**Format:** Loom screen-share of AMP Atlas
**Length:** ~3–4 min
**Goal:** Viewer understands that each piece of work is its own Draft, how to create/name/switch
between Drafts in one System, and that each Draft is published independently.

---

## Before you record

- 💡 Have a System open with **at least two Drafts** already created (clearly named, e.g.
  "Onboarding email refresh" and "LAUNCH rubric fix") so switching is real.
- 💡 Make a small edit in each ahead of time so switching visibly shows different content.

---

## The script

### 0. Cold open ⏱️ ~20s
**🖱️ On screen:** A System open, currently in one Draft.
**🎙️ Say:** "Real work is never just one thing at a time. In this video I'll show you how to
have several pieces of work going at once in the same System — without them getting tangled
up. The trick is that each piece of work lives in its own Draft."

### 1. One workstream = one Draft ⏱️ ~30s
**🖱️ On screen:** Point at the current Draft name in the status bar.
**🎙️ Say:** "Remember, a Draft is your private, self-contained copy. So the simple rule is:
one piece of work, one Draft. They don't touch each other — a change over here never leaks
into a change over there. That's also how a whole team works in the same System at once:
everyone's in their own Draft."

### 2. Create a second Draft ⏱️ ~40s
**🖱️ On screen:**
- Open the version switcher.
- Create a **new Draft**; give it a clear, specific name.

**🎙️ Say:** "Say I'm mid-way through one thing and something else comes up. I don't have to
finish first — I'll just open the version switcher and start a **new Draft**, and give it a
name that says what it's for. Clear names really matter once you've got a few going — that's
how you'll tell them apart."

### 3. Switch between Drafts ⏱️ ~45s
**🖱️ On screen:**
- Open the switcher; show the list of Drafts.
- Switch to the other Draft; show the content change.
- Point at the status bar confirming which Draft you're in.

**🎙️ Say:** "Jumping between them is easy — I open the switcher, and here are all my Drafts. I
click into this one… and AMP Atlas shows me *that* Draft's version of the System. The status
bar always tells me which Draft I'm in, so before I start typing, I just glance up to make sure
I'm in the right one. Good habit."

### 4. Where new Drafts start from ⏱️ ~30s
**🖱️ On screen:** In the New Draft window, point at the "start from" area.
**🎙️ Say:** "One thing to know: every new Draft starts fresh from the **Live Version** — the
latest official work. That keeps each Draft simple and predictable. You might see an 'Advanced'
option to build on another Draft — that's not turned on yet, so for now, Drafts always begin
from the Live Version."

### 5. Publish them independently ⏱️ ~30s
**🖱️ On screen:** Gesture across the two Drafts in the switcher.
**🎙️ Say:** "And the best part: each Draft goes through review and gets published totally on
its own schedule. Finishing one doesn't touch the others — they just sit here waiting for you.
You're never forced to wrap up everything at once."

### 6. Wrap ⏱️ ~15s
**🖱️ On screen:** Rest on the switcher or the System.
**🎙️ Say:** "So that's juggling multiple projects: one Draft per piece of work, switch between
them from up here, and publish each when it's ready. Thanks for watching!"

---

## If something goes wrong while recording

- **Both Drafts look identical** when you switch — you didn't pre-load different edits; set that
  up before recording so the switch is visible.
- **You mis-click into the Live Version** — point out the "Read only" pill and use it as a
  teachable moment, then switch back into a Draft.
